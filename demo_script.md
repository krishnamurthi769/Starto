# Starto Demo Script

## 1. The Hook (0-30s)
"Hi, I'm [Name]. 90% of startups fail because they launch in the wrong place at the wrong time. 
Meet **Starto**, the AI co-founder that validates your business idea in seconds.
Instead of guessing, Starto uses real-time location data and GPT-4 to tell you exactly *where* and *how* to launch."

## 2. The Walkthrough (30s-90s)
**[Show Home Page]**
"We start here. Clean, simple. I'm a founder wanting to open a 'Cafe'."

**[Click 'Start Analysis' -> Dashboard]**
"I'm taken to the dashboard. I select 'Cafe' and drop a pin on this busy street in New York."
**[Click Map]**

**[Show Analysis]**
"Instantly, Starto analyzes the area.
- **Demand Score**: 85/100. High foot traffic.
- **Competition**: Moderate.
- **Risk**: Low.

*(Note to Judges: For this MVP, we use mock data to demonstrate the logic. In production, this connects to live Google Places and Census APIs for real-time accuracy.)*"

**[Highlight AI Insight]**
"But here's the magic. Our AI doesn't just give numbers. It says: 'High demand but strong coffee competition. Consider a niche like bubble tea or a bakery to stand out.' That's actionable advice."

**[Show Resources]**
"And to get started? Starto lists nearby freelancers and available workspaces right here. Everything I need to go from idea to execution, in one platform."

## 3. Closing
"Starto isn't just a tool; it's a launchpad. Thank you."
